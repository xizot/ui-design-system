## How To Apply The Theory

Use theory as a decision filter, not as academic explanation in final UI. In practice:

- color decisions become token and semantic tone choices
- grid theory becomes layout, alignment, grouping, and responsive behavior
- typography theory becomes hierarchy and scanning rules
- UX psychology becomes state coverage, feedback, error prevention, and clear actions
- design-system theory becomes API boundaries, variants, and extraction rules

Do not quote or reproduce book content. Keep the skill actionable and self-contained.
